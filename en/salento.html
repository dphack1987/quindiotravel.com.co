🏛 Salento: The Heritage Town of Quindío

Founded in 1842, Salento is considered one of Colombia's most beautiful towns, famous for its colorful balconies, traditional architecture, and access to Valle de Cocora.

🏘️ Traditional Architecture

Colorful bahareque houses with flower-filled balconies and red-tiled roofs

☕ Coffee Culture

Gateway to Colombia's finest coffee farms and experiences

🌴 Valle de Cocora

Home to Colombia's national tree - the towering wax palms

🎨 Handicrafts
